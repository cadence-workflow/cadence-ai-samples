# Session outline

We'll start with Incident Triage to get familiar with the agent and Cadence.
Inspect an intentional lookup failure, adjust its retry policy and see the
agent continue with a real model.

Then choose what you'd like to work on: extend an AI sample, bring a small
workflow to work on with a maintainer, or take another available Cadence Issue.

We'll leave time to share progress and save the next step, whether that's a
recipe, test, draft pull request or handoff.
