# Session outline

1. **Welcome and setup check.** Make sure Cadence, Ollama and `qwen2.5:7b` are
   running. If your laptop can't run the model, pair with someone.
2. **Retry-policy exercise, everyone together.** Watch the agent investigate in
   Cadence Web, see a lookup fail, fix its retry policy and run it again.
3. **Pick one ContribFest Issue or bring your own agent.** The Issues and how to
   claim them are in [Find something to work on](shortlist.md). Maintainers
   move between tables.
4. **Share and hand off.** Show what you got working. Open a pull request or
   write down the next step.
